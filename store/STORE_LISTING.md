**App name:** One Second

**Short description (80 chars max):**
Keep one small moment each day. Turn the month into a story.

**Full description:**
One Second helps you keep a little piece of today.

Each day, keep one small moment — a photo, a short video, or a few words. Add a caption and how the day felt if you like. Missed a day? That's fine. There are no streak penalties and no pressure.

At the end of the month, your moments come together as a story you can play back, and a month card you can share.

• One moment a day — simple, calm, no pressure
• Photo, short video, or a written note
• A calendar of your memories
• A story for every month
• A gentle daily reminder, only if you want it
• Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your moments stay on your phone.
