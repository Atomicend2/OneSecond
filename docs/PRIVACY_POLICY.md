# One Second — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

One Second ("the app") is a personal daily-moment journal.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

**What is stored on your device:** the photos, videos, notes, captions and moods you choose to keep, plus your reminder preference. This data is stored privately inside the app on your device only. It is not backed up to any cloud by the app.

**Permissions:** the app asks for notification permission only if you turn on the daily reminder. Photos and videos are taken with your device's camera app or chosen with the system photo picker, so the app does not request camera or storage access.

**Sharing:** content leaves your device only when you tap Share and choose where to send it. That transfer is handled by your device and the app you choose.

**Deleting your data:** delete any moment from its page, or delete everything from Settings → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

**Children:** the app is not directed at children under 13.

**Changes:** if we add optional features such as accounts or cloud backup in the future, we will update this policy and describe exactly what is collected before it is used.
